forgiving UI (033), voice-first UI (034, narrowed to conversational

- **Haptics as a standalone technique** — researched in Drop 3 and held

  (Android haptics principles, Apple HIG haptics mirror); revisit if a
  haptic device ever joins the lineup.
- **Standalone AR/VR** — researched in Drop 3 as look-and-pinch / gaze
  targets (60pt gaze targets, dwell and pinch confirmation) and held for
  a future drop: no headset in his lineup, and the portable lesson (big
  targets, confirm before you act) is small next to the rest. Draft and
  sources exist; the visionOS widget angle already lives in entry 021
  (glanceable surfaces).
