# 015 — Accessibility presets

> Entry 015 — added 2026-11-13 (Drop 2: Games, editors, and gadgets).

*one tap, a whole comfort setup*

**What it is.** Instead of a settings maze with forty toggles, the app
offers named presets at first boot: Vision, Hearing, Motor, Motion
Sickness. One tap switches on a whole group of settings together. LEGO
Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight (launched May 22, 2026) puts four
presets on first launch. Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced (launched
July 9, 2026) puts accessibility presets in the first-boot flow and in
the options menu, with groups of settings toggled on and off together
with a single press. Apple has gone further: Accessibility Nutrition
Labels now appear on App Store product pages, so support is visible
before install.

**Why it feels good.** The person who needs bigger text does not want a
research project. They want to tap once and play. A preset says: we
already thought about this, here is a setup that works, change any piece
of it later if you want. It turns accessibility from a checklist buried
in settings into a welcome.

**The numbers.**
- LEGO Batman first-launch presets: Vision, Hearing, Motor, Motion
  Sickness. One Touch Interactions turns most hold interactions and
  repeated taps into a single button tap. Game speed options: 100, 90,
  75, and 50 percent. Subtitles in three sizes.
- Black Flag Resynced: subtitles in a Largest size of 46 px. Aim
  Assistance in four settings: off, light, moderate, full. Quick-time
  events skippable. Colorblindness presets. HUD settings changeable all
  at once or individually.
- Apple Accessibility Nutrition Labels appear on App Store product pages
  in every country where the app is available, and the section still
  shows if the developer has not specified support. The European
  Accessibility Act began applying on June 28, 2025.

**Where it fits his apps.** Every app, but start with the ones a tired
person uses at night: Yahtzee (a big-text preset, one tap), STELLA (a
reduced-motion preset), Code Forge (a high-contrast preset), MIMIK (a
one-touch capture preset). A preset is a starting point, not a wall:
every setting inside it must still be adjustable on its own.

**Watch out.** Do not bury presets three menus deep. First boot is where
they belong, with a plain-words name for each one. And do not guess at
what a preset should contain; group the settings people actually change
together, and let the user fine-tune after.
