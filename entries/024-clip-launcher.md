# 024 — Clip launcher

> Entry 024 — added 2026-11-13 (Drop 2: Games, editors, and gadgets).

*tap a pad, it plays*

**What it is.** A grid of clips you fire in any order, in real time,
while everything keeps playing. That is Ableton Live's Session View, and
it is the reason Live exists: the software was born in 2001 out of the
idea that you should not have to stop the music in order to make music.
You drop a clip, launch it, layer another on top, and a track assembles
itself under your hands before you have consciously planned anything.
The grid is the instrument. Live 12.4 shipped May 5, 2026, still built
around the same view. Cubase, by contrast, has no clip launcher at all:
it is a linear timeline, and that is a deliberate fork in the road.

**Why it feels good.** There is no gap between idea and sound. You do
not configure, then press play, then listen. You tap, and it plays.
The grid rewards impulse: idea, clip, launch, and the software gets out
of your way. It is the closest a screen gets to feeling like an
instrument you can play, because it is one.

**The numbers.** No timing or size number in the source. What it gives
instead is the shape of the thing: a grid of clips, fired in any order,
in real time, while everything keeps playing. Live 12.4 released May 5,
2026. Live has worked this way since 2001, and the Session View is
still described as the industry standard for live performance and fast
sketching.

**Where it fits his apps.** LYNX SHARE first and foremost: the session
view is the DAW's front door, and a DAW without a launch grid makes the
user stop the music to make music. The principle also fits anywhere a
grid of things should fire instead of a menu of things to configure:
MIMIK's capture presets as launch pads, Pixel Forge's one-tap actions
as a pad grid. But be honest about it: the grid earns its place where
firing is the job, not everywhere.

**Watch out.** The grid must stay in time. A clip launched mid-bar that
starts instantly instead of on the beat sounds broken, not responsive.
Every pad needs a clear state: empty, loaded, playing, queued. And do
not put destructive actions on pads. A pad you tap by feel is the wrong
place for anything you cannot undo.
