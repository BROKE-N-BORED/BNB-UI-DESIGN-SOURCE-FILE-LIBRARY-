# 018 — Scrub through time

> Entry 018 — added 2026-11-13 (Drop 2: Games, editors, and gadgets).

*hover or drag to see inside*

**What it is.** A thumbnail that is not a picture but a window. In
Premiere Pro's Project panel, hovering over a clip thumbnail scrubs
through its contents without clicking: move left to right and you travel
from Media Start to Media End. This is Hover Scrub. You can set In and
Out points while scrubbing, and double-clicking loads the clip into the
Source Monitor at the hovered frame. Moving the cursor away resets the
thumbnail to its poster frame. The clip tells you what is inside it
before you open it.

**Why it feels good.** Opening a clip to find out what it is costs a
click, a wait, and a context switch. Scrubbing costs a hover. You can
check ten clips in the time it takes to open one. It turns browsing from
a series of commitments into a glance.

**The numbers.** No timing number in the source. What it gives instead is
the interaction, and it is exact: Shift + H turns it off, hold Shift to
enable it temporarily when it is off, I and O
set In and Out points while scrubbing, double-click loads at the hovered
frame, moving away resets to the poster frame, and Hover Scrub does not
play audio. Poster frame: Shift + P.

**Where it fits his apps.** Pixel Forge (scrub a video clip in the strip
without opening it), MIMIK (scrub a capture to find the moment), LYNX
SHARE (scrub a waveform to hear where the part starts), ACR (scrub the
trace log timeline to find when resonance dropped).

**Watch out.** Scrubbing needs thumbnails, and thumbnails need to exist
before the hover. On a spinning drive, generate them once and cache
them, or the scrub stutters and the whole trick falls apart. No audio
on hover is a feature, not a bug: scrubbing ten clips should not play
ten sounds at once.
