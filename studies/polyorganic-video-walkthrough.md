# PolyOrganic launch video, frame by frame

> Study, added 2026-11-14. The launch demo preserved in writing: every action, every value, every material, in order.

*Fifty seconds, three acts: a plank, a floor, and 31 looks on one cube.*

## The video

TikTok, @polyplaygroundhub, about 50 seconds, Blender viewport throughout. The same demo was posted to Instagram on September 22, 2026 as the launch announcement (8.4K likes, 160 comments), and again September 25 with narration. The TikTok post date is unconfirmed. The video has no text overlays beyond the Blender UI and the TikTok watermark; the end card is the TikTok logo with a search for @polyplaygroundhub. All timestamps below are seconds into the video. Values were read from extracted frames, checked at half-second intervals through the fast cycling in Act 3.

## How to read this

Breadcrumbs are quoted exactly as shown, with middle dots: "Wood · White Oak · Solid". Center dial values are the value shown when the frame was taken; the presenter drags knobs continuously, so a value is a moment, not a setting. The dial's inner ring pages are Material, Style, Layout, Detail, Form, Surface, Life, Colour, Depth, Saved. The three buttons under the dial are always Surprise, Save, Close. The knob above the breadcrumb is read here as the current page's first control; see the open question in the main study.

## Act 1: the plank (0 to 20 seconds)

- 0.0: A black cube on the grid. The dial is open. Breadcrumb: "Blank". Center dial: Seed 0.0.
- 1.0: Material page. Blank is selected. The cursor hovers Wood. Breadcrumb reads "Blank · Oak". The cube is still bare grey.
- 2.0: Style page. White Oak selected. Breadcrumb: "Wood · White Oak · Solid". The cube is light wood, end grain visible on the right face. The Style ring shows 15 wood species: Oak, White Oak, Walnut, Maple, Cherry, Ash, Pine, Teak, Mahogany, Ebony, Blackbutt, Spotted Gum, Jarrah, Tasmanian Oak, Weathered.
- 3.1: Ash hovered, then Walnut. Breadcrumb changes with the hover: "Wood · Ash · Solid", then "Wood · Walnut · Solid". The cube changes colour with each hover.
- 4 to 6: Back on White Oak. The presenter scales the cube flat along Z into a long plank. The wood grain stretches with the object.
- 7.2: Detail page. Center dial: Ring Spacing 6.0 mm. The Detail ring holds Ring Spacing, Figure, Grain Lines, Pores, Knots.
- 8.2: Detail page. Center dial: Figure 56%.
- 9.2: Form page. Center dial: Structure 100%. The Form ring holds Structure, Scale, Flow, Variation, Seed. The Scale knob reads x0.20.
- 10.2: Form page. Center dial: Scale x0.26.
- 11.3: Form page. Center dial: Flow 2%.
- 12.3: Surface page. Center dial: Gloss 23%. The Surface ring holds Gloss, Metal, Coat, Sheen, Translucency.
- 13.3: Surface page. Center dial: Coat 25%.
- 14.4: Life page. Center dial: Grime 100%. The Life ring holds Edge Wear, Edge Width, Exposed Metal, Dirt, Dust, Grime, Wet.
- 16.4: Life page. Center dial: Dirt 100%, the orange arc nearly full.
- 17.4: Colour page. The center dial becomes a hue wheel: Hue A 34 degrees. The Colour ring holds seven knobs: Hue A, Sat A, Tone A, Sat B, Tone B, Exposed, Dirt Tone.
- 18.5: Depth page. Center dial: Depth 26%. The Depth ring mixes control types: a Depth knob, Smart Limits set to Auto, Relief and Micro knobs, a Real Geometry toggle, quality buttons (Draft, Balanced, Final; Balanced active), World (Wld) and Obj projection buttons, and a 1:1 button.
- 19 to 20: Back on the Material page. The plank is done: weathered white oak, dirt at full.

## Act 2: the floor (20 to 28 seconds)

- 21.0 to 21.2: A cut. The dial closes and a fresh plane sits under the plank. The Add menu itself flashes by too fast to read; the plane is already there at 21.2.
- 21.3: The plane is scaled up to become the floor (status bar reads Scale 4.16 on all axes, then 4.76). The empty state stays on screen throughout.
- 21.5: The dial on the new plane shows the empty state. Breadcrumb: "PolyOrganic · Plane". The circle reads "No PolyOrganic material here" with "Click to start" in orange. A Close button sits below.
- 22.6: Wood applied to the plane. Breadcrumb: "Wood · Oak · Boards". The plane is a light board floor. The plank from Act 1 lies on top of it.
- 23.6: Layout page. Walnut, Parquet. Breadcrumb: "Wood · Walnut · Parquet".
- 24.6: Layout page. Herringbone selected. Breadcrumb: "Wood · Walnut · Herringbone". The Layout ring holds Solid, Boards, Parquet, Chevron, Herringbone, Strip. Center dial: Depth 0%.
- 25.6: Form page. Center dial: Scale x0.20.
- 26.7: Life page. Center dial: Edge Width 9.8 mm. The floor's top half has gone pale grey where the wear bites.
- 27.7: Life page. Center dial: Grime 100%. The whole floor is pale and dusty now.

## Act 3: 31 looks on one cube (28 to 47 seconds)

- 28.7: A rendered close-up with no UI at all: two planks on the herringbone floor. A breath before the last act.
- 29.0: The dial opens with an area light selected. Breadcrumb: "PolyOrganic · PolyStudio_Area_Light". The circle reads "Select a mesh to begin". A second empty state: the dial knows the selection is not a mesh.
- 29.3: Add menu, Mesh submenu open. The menu holds Search, Mesh, Curve, Surface, Metaball, Text, Point Cloud, Volume, Grease Pencil, Armature, Lattice, Empty, Image, Light, Light Probe, Camera, Speaker, Force Field, Collection Instance. The Mesh submenu holds Plane, Cube, Circle, UV Sphere, Ico Sphere, Cylinder, Cone, Torus, Grid, Monkey, Rock Generator, Single Vert, Round Cube, Torus Objects, Math Function, Gears, Pipe Joints, Diamonds, Extras, Parent to Empty, Landscape, Bolt, Archimesh. The cursor sits on UV Sphere.
- 29.7 to 30.8: A cube is added, with rounded (beveled) edges. Empty state again. Breadcrumb: "PolyOrganic · Cube.002". "No PolyOrganic material here. Click to start."

Then the cycling. One click per material, about half a second each, the cube changing live. Breadcrumbs in order, read from frames at half-second intervals. That is 32 stops including the opening Blank: 31 named looks, 30 of them distinct (Linen appears twice).

Notes on the cycling:

- The center dial reads Grime through most of the cycle: 0% at Gloss Plastic, 25% from Carrara through Raw, 45% at Exposed Aggregate, 35% from Red Face through Brown. The last two Ground looks read Structure 100%. The Grime value steps between cuts, which means the video is cut from more than one pass, or the presenter adjusted Grime between looks.
- At 44.5 the Detail page opens on the brown leather: Grain Size 58% in the center dial, with a Creases knob below it. Leather's Detail knobs are its own, not wood's: no Ring Spacing here.
- Matte Paint appears in the Blank style ring but is never selected in the sampled frames.
- The Style rings seen in full: Blank (Matte Paint, Gloss Plastic, Rubber, Ceramic, Chrome, Gold, Chipped Paint; Ceramic is partly hidden by the TikTok watermark), Marble (Carrara, Calacatta, Verde, Nero Marquina, Rosso), Stone (Sandstone, Slate, Bluestone, Granite, Limestone, River Cobbles), Concrete (Raw, Polished, Aged, Exposed Aggregate), Leather (Tan, Brown, Black, Oxblood), Ground (Lawn, Lawn and Mud, Dry Grass, Moss, Mud, Soil, Sand; Moss is partly hidden by the watermark).
- The brick Style ring is never sampled in full; only Red Face and London Stock are seen as selected styles.

## The catalog observed

Everything the video shows, in one place:

- Material families, 13: Blank, Wood, Marble, Stone, Concrete, Brick, Tile, Fabric, Twill, Carpet, Brushed, Leather, Ground. Twill and Brushed are families in their own right, not styles of Fabric and Metal.
- Wood styles, 15: Oak, White Oak, Walnut, Maple, Cherry, Ash, Pine, Teak, Mahogany, Ebony, Blackbutt, Spotted Gum, Jarrah, Tasmanian Oak, Weathered.
- Layouts: wood (Solid, Boards, Parquet, Chevron, Herringbone, Strip), brick (Stretcher, Stack, English, Flemish, Herringbone, Basket Weave), stone (Ashlar, Cobbles, Crazy Paving), tile (Offset). Layout names repeat across families (Herringbone for wood and brick); the ring adapts per family.
- Knobs by page. Detail, wood: Ring Spacing, Figure, Grain Lines, Pores, Knots. Detail, leather: Grain Size, Creases. Form: Structure, Scale, Flow, Variation, Seed. Surface: Gloss, Metal, Coat, Sheen, Translucency. Life: Edge Wear, Edge Width, Exposed Metal, Dirt, Dust, Grime, Wet. Colour: Hue A, Sat A, Tone A, Sat B, Tone B, Exposed, Dirt Tone. Depth: Depth, Smart Limits (Auto), Relief, Micro, Real Geometry, Draft/Balanced/Final, Wld/Obj, 1:1.
- Values seen on the center dial: Seed 0.0, Ring Spacing 6.0 mm, Figure 56%, Structure 100%, Scale x0.20 and x0.26, Flow 2%, Gloss 23%, Coat 25%, Grime 0%, 25%, 35%, 45%, 100%, Dirt 100%, Hue A 34 degrees, Depth 0% and 26%, Edge Width 9.8 mm, Grain Size 58%.

## What the video never shows

The Surprise button pressed, the Save button pressed, the Saved page opened, Simple vs Advanced mode, resizing or moving the dial, type-to-search, Ctrl+click paste, the bake, layers, roofing, or the node tree. Those live on the product page or in the other reels; see the main study.
